Our Studio

Crafting Interiors

Élume Interiors is a Bangalore-based premium design studio where architecture meets emotion. We specialize in residential and commercial spaces that are beautiful in form and flawless in function.

Our multidisciplinary team — architects, stylists, and craftsmen — collaborates on every project from first sketch to final reveal, ensuring a seamless, joyful experience for every client.
